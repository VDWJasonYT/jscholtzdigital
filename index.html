FAQ

Who do you work with? Medical and dental practices across South Africa: GPs, dentists, physios, specialists and clinics.

What is an AI video creative? A short video ad for Facebook and Instagram that we create with AI video tools, based on your practice. You approve every creative before it runs.

How much do the packages cost? Practice Starter is R9,500 setup then R8,500/mo, Practice Growth is R18,000 setup then R9,500/mo, and Practice Premium is R38,000 setup then R15,000/mo. Every package has a 50% deposit on setup, the first month paid upfront and a 3-month minimum.

What do custom practice apps cost? Custom practice apps start from R80,000 and are quoted per project. That includes work such as a patient booking or reminder app, or a practice portal.

What do Basic Care and Secure Care include? Basic Care covers hosting checks, updates, backups and small text edits. Secure Care is everything in Basic Care, plus a monthly security scan, malware cleanup and priority support. Practice Starter includes Basic Care. Practice Growth and Practice Premium include Secure Care.

Is ad spend included? No. Ad spend is always extra and paid by you straight to Meta. We suggest at least R3,000 a month.

Do you guarantee leads or sales? No. Nobody can honestly guarantee booked patients. We agree clear goals with you, track them, and report on what ran in plain language.

Can you take over our existing practice website or ad account? Yes. We first check that the practice owns and can access the site and accounts. Then we explain what to keep, what to fix, and whether a rebuild is the better path.

What is a website security check? A passive review of your site from the outside, written up in plain English. We only do active testing if you give us written permission first.

Where are you based? We are based in Secunda, Mpumalanga, and work remote-first with medical and dental practices across South Africa.
