FAQ

Who do you work with? Small businesses anywhere that need a website that brings in enquiries. Our live work covers a dental practice, a holiday house, a school and a logistics company.

Do you work with clients outside South Africa? Yes. We are based in South Africa and work remote-first, so calls, design reviews and launch all happen online. Prices show in US dollars outside South Africa.

How much does a website cost? A one-page website starts from R4,500 and a multi-page website from R12,000. Custom web apps start from R80,000 and are quoted per project. Monthly packages start at R9,500 setup then R8,500/mo.

What do Basic Care and Secure Care include? Basic Care covers hosting checks, updates, backups and small text edits. Secure Care is everything in Basic Care, plus a monthly security scan, malware cleanup and priority support.

Is ad spend included? No. Ad spend is always extra and paid by you straight to Meta. We suggest at least R3,000 a month.

Do you guarantee leads or sales? No. Nobody can honestly guarantee sales. We agree clear goals with you, track them, and report on what ran in plain language.

Can you take over our existing website or ad account? Yes. We first check that you own and can access the site and accounts. Then we explain what to keep, what to fix, and whether a rebuild is the better path.

What is a website security check? A passive review of your site from the outside, written up in plain English. We only do active testing if you give us written permission first.

What is an AI video creative? A short video ad for Facebook and Instagram that we create with AI video tools, based on your business. You approve every creative before it runs.
