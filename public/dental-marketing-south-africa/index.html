Dental marketing for practices in South Africa

I'm Jason Scholtz. When a dental practice asks for marketing, I mean a specific set of jobs, not a vague promise of growth. I am based in Secunda and I work with practices across South Africa.

The work I actually do

  • Look after the practice website, including text changes you ask for.
  • Set up and maintain the Google Business Profile so patients can find the practice on Search and Maps, with a link to reviews the practice already has.
  • Build enquiry and booking paths that match the diary.
  • Make short ads for Facebook and Instagram. You approve every creative before it runs.

Ad spend is not my fee. You pay Meta directly. I am not putting prices on this page. The homepage is where the packages are listed.

What I will not promise

I do not guarantee leads, bookings or sales. Nobody honest can. We agree what to track, and I tell you what ran in plain language.

I will not run a claim about treatment results that you cannot stand behind. The HPCSA sets advertising rules for practitioners in South Africa. I am not a lawyer and I do not sign a campaign off as compliant. You approve the words first.

Most practices start with the website itself. That is the dental website design page. How enquiry details are handled is on the POPIA page, which is not legal advice. The site I can point to today is Modern Dental.

Do you guarantee new patients from ads?

No. I agree what we will look at, and I report what ran. I do not guarantee leads or sales.

Is the ad budget part of your fee?

No. You pay Meta directly for the ad spend. That spend is separate from my fee.

Who approves the ads?

You do. I do not run a creative until you have approved it.
