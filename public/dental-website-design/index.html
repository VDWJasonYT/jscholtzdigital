Dental website design for practices in South Africa

I'm Jason Scholtz. I design websites for dental practices. I live in Secunda, and I work with practices across South Africa.

A dental site I build is straightforward. It says who the dentist is, where the rooms are, and how a patient gets in touch. Call, WhatsApp and a simple enquiry are the paths I start with, because that is how most people actually reach a practice.

I build the pages mobile-first. A patient on a phone should not have to pinch and hunt for the number.

What I have already shipped

The public example is Modern Dental, Dr Christo Brecher's practice in Secunda. The live site is moderndental.co.za. That page lists what is actually on the site. I am not going to add a result I have not measured.

What I will and will not say

I do not guarantee new patients, leads or a full book. A website makes the next step obvious for someone who already found you. It is not a machine that creates appointments on its own.

Health practitioners in South Africa advertise under rules set by the HPCSA. I am not a lawyer, and I do not certify that a page complies. I will not put a treatment claim on your site that you have not approved.

If you also want help with the Google Business Profile or with ads, that is separate work. I describe it on the dental marketing page. You pay the ad platform directly for any spend. I do not quote a fee on this page.

Enquiry forms I set up ask the person to agree before the message goes. How I handle those details is on the POPIA page. That page is marked as not legal advice. A GP, physio, specialist or clinic may be a closer fit for the medical practice page.

Do you only design dental websites in Secunda?

No. I am based in Secunda. I work with dental practices across South Africa.

Will the website bring new patients?

I can't promise that. The site should make it easy for someone who found the practice to call or send a message. That is not a booking guarantee.

Can you change a site the practice already has?

Yes, if the practice owns it and can give me access. I will say what I would keep and what I would change before any rebuild.
