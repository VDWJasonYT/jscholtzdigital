Medical practice website design in South Africa

I'm Jason Scholtz. I design websites for medical and dental practices. I am based in Secunda, Mpumalanga, and the work is remote-first.

The practices I work with are GPs, dentists, physios, specialists and clinics. Most of the day they are with patients. The site has a small job while they are in the rooms: say who you are, where you are, and how a patient reaches you.

What the site needs to do

  • Read clearly on a phone.
  • Show the practice name, the town, and a way to call.
  • Offer WhatsApp where the practice actually uses it.
  • Give an enquiry path that matches how the rooms work, from a short form to something built around the diary.
  • Include a privacy note and a consent step on the enquiry. The POPIA page explains the limit of that. It is not legal advice.

I do not guarantee that a new site will fill the appointment book. It is the usual starting point, not a promise.

Dentists can read the more specific dental website page. The one site I can show in public is Modern Dental in Secunda. Ongoing profile and ads work is described under dental marketing, and it applies the same way when a medical practice asks for it. I am not listing prices here.

I will not publish a clinical claim you have not approved. Advertising rules for practitioners in South Africa are set by the HPCSA. I am not the person who certifies compliance.

Which practices do you build sites for?

GPs, dentists, physios, specialists and clinics across South Africa. A dental-only brief is also covered on the dental website page.

No. I do not guarantee leads or booked patients.
