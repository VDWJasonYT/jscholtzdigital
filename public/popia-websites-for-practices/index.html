POPIA and websites for medical and dental practices

This is not legal advice. I am not a lawyer or a compliance officer. This page describes how I handle enquiries on jscholtzdigital.com, and what I can put on a practice site I build. It is general information. For advice about your own practice, speak to a lawyer, or to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

I'm Jason Scholtz, in Secunda. Practices sometimes ask whether I can make their website "POPIA compliant". I can't give you that label. POPIA is about how personal information is processed. A website is only one piece. What the practice does with a patient's details after they arrive is the practice's responsibility.

What I do on my own site

  • The contact form cannot be sent until the person ticks: "I agree that JScholtz Digital may use these details only to reply to this enquiry, in line with POPIA."
  • The form does not store the message on a server. It opens the person's own email or WhatsApp with the details filled in, and they choose whether to send.
  • There is a field people are not meant to see, used to catch automated submissions. I do not treat that as a security guarantee.
  • The privacy policy says what I collect: business name, website if you share one, WhatsApp number, email, and anything you type. I use it to reply, to prepare a quote, and to do the work you asked for. I do not sell it.
  • Enquiries that do not become clients are deleted within 12 months. That is written in the privacy policy.
  • You can ask me to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop. The privacy policy says how, and it links to the Information Regulator.

What I can add to a practice site

On a site I build for a practice, I can include a privacy page and a consent step before an enquiry is sent. The practice approves the wording. I do not decide that those two things make the practice compliant.

What I will not do

  • I will not tell patients or colleagues that your practice is POPIA compliant because the site has a privacy page.
  • I will not draft consent forms for treatment. That is legal work.
  • I will not give an HPCSA opinion. Advertising rules are a different question, and I do not certify them.

If you want a practice site that includes a privacy page and a consent step, start with dental website design or medical practice website design.

Does a privacy page make a practice POPIA compliant?

No. I will not say that. A page and a consent tick are only one part of how a practice handles personal information.
