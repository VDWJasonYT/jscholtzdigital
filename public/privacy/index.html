Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are

JScholtz Digital is a digital marketing agency based in Secunda, Mpumalanga, South Africa. This page explains how we handle personal information when you contact us through our website or our ads.

What we collect

When you fill in a form on our site, or a lead form on our ads, we collect: your business name; your website, if you share one; your WhatsApp number; your email address; anything else you choose to type in a message.

Why we collect it

We use these details to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and provide the services you ask for. We do not sell your information.

Who sees it

Your information is seen by our team, and by the service providers we need to run the site and forms, for example Meta for lead ads and our hosting provider. They only see what they need to do that work.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become clients are deleted within 12 months. If you become a client, we keep your records for as long as we need them to provide the service, and for as long as tax or other legal requirements say we must.

Your rights under POPIA

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information. You can also object, or opt out of marketing, at any time. Reply STOP on WhatsApp, or email us, and we will stop.

If you are not satisfied, you have the right to complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at https://inforegulator.org.za/.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to access, correct or delete your information, can be sent to mrjasonscholtz@gmail.com or WhatsApp +27 61 599 0085.
