About the room

A hall built for a night like this

Graceland Arena in Secunda is a 30m by 41.3m indoor hall with a 9m ceiling, at 1 eMbalenhle Road. This demo maps 1,000 seats around a stage at one end, with Golden Circle at the front, Arena blocks A, B and C, and a rear block D.

Prices are placeholders you can edit in one config file: Golden Circle at R450, Arena at R350, Rear at R250. Nothing here takes real money. Checkout uses the PayFast sandbox only.

This page is unlisted. It is a design and checkout test, not an endorsement, and not a confirmed booking with Will Linley or Peermont Graceland.
